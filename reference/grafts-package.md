# grafts: Graph Forecasts for Time Series

Interactive exploration of graph-temporal data, such as hierarchical and
grouped time series, and their forecasts. The series graph and the graph
of key combinations are drawn in a browser app, beside plots of the
series reached and their forecasts from a fable.
