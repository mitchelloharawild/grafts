# grafts (development version)

* `grafts()` makes an explorer of an aggregated tsibble, and optionally its forecasts, drawn in the browser through its series and dimension graphs. Where it goes decides how the series reach the browser: printed in the console, it is served from the R session a series at a time; knitted, in Shiny (`renderGrafts()`) or saved with `grafts_save()`, every series is written into the page, which needs no R process. `grafts_save()`, `api_grafts()` and `grafts_serve()` take an explorer, or data and the arguments of `grafts()`.
* `grafts_options()` (the `options` argument of `grafts()`) chooses the views offered (the first being shown at the start), the theme, the seasonal period (or none), and whether visitors' choices persist. Whether series add up to their parents is detected, so the Stacked view and share scales are dropped for means and the like.
* `api_grafts()` adds the explorer to a plumber2 api, optionally under a `path`, so it can be deployed (Docker, Posit Connect) or mounted next to other routes. The path without its trailing slash redirects to it. `/api/series` rejects requests for more than `max_ids` series and responses are cacheable.
* `grafts_serve()` runs an explorer on its own server, for scripts and containers. It announces its url before blocking, and shows a loopback url when `host` is `"0.0.0.0"`.
* A page can hold several explorers, each saving its own layout and theme.
