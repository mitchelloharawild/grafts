# grafts (development version)

* `grafts()` makes an explorer of an aggregated tsibble, and optionally its forecasts, drawn in the browser through its series and dimension graphs. Where it goes decides how the series reach the browser: printed in the console, it is served from the R session a series at a time, blocking like a Shiny app until stopped; knitted, in Shiny (`renderGrafts()`) or saved with `grafts_save()`, every series is written into the page, which needs no R process. `grafts_save()`, `grafts_api()` and `grafts_serve()` take an explorer from `grafts()`.
* `grafts_options()` (the `options` argument of `grafts()`) chooses the views offered (the first being shown at the start), the theme, the seasonal period (or none), whether visitors' choices persist, how long series are sent to the app (`max_points`) the size of an embedded explorer (`width`, `height`, `element_id`) and the page `title`. Whether series add up to their parents is detected, so the Stacked view and share scales are dropped for means and the like.
* `grafts_api()` adds the explorer to a plumber2 api, optionally under a `path`, so it can be deployed (Docker, Posit Connect) or mounted next to other routes. The path without its trailing slash redirects to it. `/api/series` rejects requests for more than `max_ids` series and responses are privately cached and revalidated with an `ETag`. `/api/health` reports that it is up.
* `grafts_serve()` runs an explorer on its own server, for scripts and containers, or in the background with `block = FALSE`. It announces its url and how to stop it, and shows a loopback url when `host` is `"0.0.0.0"`.
* A page can hold several explorers, each saving its own layout and theme.
